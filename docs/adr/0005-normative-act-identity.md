# Normative Acts are identified by number; amendments stay separate Current acts

Resolução 213/2021 and 262/2025 are two Normative Acts related by Supersession, not two versions of one policy. IN 24/2023 and IN 21/2024 are both Current: 21 is an Amendment, not a replacement, and the assistant must not invent a consolidated text. Numbered identity matches how a Servidor and a Citation name the instrument; a single versioned document would hide that.
